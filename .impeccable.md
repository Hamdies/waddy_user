# Design Context — Waddi (customer app)

> Written from context confirmed by the app owner on 2026-09-06, so design skills
> have a real source instead of inferring tone from code. Update it when the
> brand direction changes.

## Target audience

Egyptian consumers ordering food, groceries and parcels on mobile. Cairo-first
(Maadi and surrounding districts at launch). Android and iOS only — no web, no
desktop. Bilingual: English and Arabic, with full RTL.

Typical context of use: one-handed, on a phone, often on a poor connection,
frequently in a hurry or hungry. Price-sensitive — discounts, savings and
delivery fees materially drive decisions.

## Use cases

- Browse restaurants and grocery stores in a delivery zone
- Add items to a cart and check out
- Track a live order
- Spots: vote weekly on places to visit, win prizes
- XP / loyalty: earn and redeem points

## Brand personality and tone

**Fresh, energetic, a bit playful.** Explicitly NOT premium, restrained or
luxury. When choosing between a bolder and a quieter treatment, **bias bolder**.

Voice is warm and direct, first-person-plural as the brand ("Waddy got you…").
Playful is welcome; cute-for-its-own-sake is not. Copy must never overclaim —
the app promises delivery times, fees and discounts that the backend then has to
honour, so accuracy outranks charm.

## Palette (lib/theme/light_theme.dart)

| Token | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| `primary` | `#134E4A` | deep teal — brand core, bars, primary surfaces |
| `mint` | `#1EF2A0` | electric mint — **rationed to pressable controls and CTAs** |
| `coral` | `#FF6B6B` | warmth, urgency, destructive |
| `ink` | `#1A1F1E` | teal-tinted near-black — never pure `#000` |
| `surface` | off-white | page/card ground — never pure `#fff` |

Neutrals are deliberately tinted toward the teal hue. Light-only by design: the
module screens paint fixed light surfaces, so a dark scaffold would render white
cards on near-black.

## Constraints

- Flutter, GetX state management
- 4pt spacing grid via `Dimensions` (see `lib/util/dimensions.dart`) — new UI
  must use tokens, not numeric literals
- Must work at ~400px width and in Arabic RTL
- Minimum tap target 48pt (`Dimensions.minTapTarget`)
- Every user-facing string needs a key in **both** `en.json` and `ar.json`; a
  missing key renders as the raw key, silently

## Differentiation

Mint on deep teal is the signature — it should read as unmistakably Waddi, not
as another green delivery app. Mint is scarce on purpose: it marks the thing you
press.
