# Proposed advertising campaigns

Campaign concepts shown in the partner brochure (pages 9–12). **None of these
has run.** No reach, impressions or order numbers exist for them and none
should be quoted. Each needs budget, creative and scheduling approval.

Targeting for all: Waddy's service area only (Maadi and confirmed surrounding
zones). Exact radius and audiences to be set in Meta Ads Manager.

## Partner Spotlight

Content Waddy produces about one participating business and publishes on
Waddy's own channels: the brand, its products, location, specialties, current
offers and a direct path to order on Waddy.

To decide: selection criteria, how many per month, and what production Waddy
provides. Until then the brochure describes Spotlight as an opportunity, not
an entitlement.

## Participation model

| Category | What it is | Who pays |
|---|---|---|
| A. Platform-led marketing | Waddy brand awareness, neighborhood and seasonal campaigns, partner discovery content, Waddy Spots, organic social | Waddy |
| B. Partner promotion | Selected partners featured in Waddy content and campaigns (e.g. Spotlight) | Waddy, by selection |
| C. Optional paid promotion | Sponsored in-app placements; partner-specific paid social campaigns | Partner, optional |

Rule the brochure commits to: a partner never has to buy advertising to
receive standard marketplace visibility.

## Measurement

Report what is measurable: reach, impressions, clicks, engagement, app visits
and attributed orders. Attribution from Meta ads into the app depends on the
tracking described in `docs/ads_tracking_plan.md`; until that is live,
attributed orders cannot be reported and should not be promised.

## Before running any creative

- The app-store listing rules still apply: no competitor names, no delivery-time
  claims, no ratings that are not real.
- Spots prize draws need the sponsor note ("Waddy runs this draw…") wherever
  the draw is advertised.
- Get written approval from a partner before using their name, logo or food
  photography in a paid ad.
- The brochure's sample creatives use placeholder names ("Your Brand") and the
  app's own illustration art. Replace with real partner photography.
